VOIDRUNNER v0.1

Cyberpunk isometric RPG for M5Stack Tab5 (ESP32-P4).
Flash straight from Chrome or Edge over USB-C.

Your browser can't flash here — use desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial required). Web Serial needs a secure (https) page — load this over https.
Download VoidRunner-tab5-merged.bin (full image, offset 0x0)

Plug the Tab5 into your computer with USB-C, click Connect & Flash, and pick the Tab5's serial port. If no port appears, hold the BOOT button while connecting, then try again. Flashing erases the current firmware and installs VoidRunner.
